### 10.1 
> [! Abstract] Let $x\in G,$ a finite group. $|G|=n.$ Let $ord_G(x)\coloneqq d\mid n.$ Then $ord(x^m)=\frac{d}{(m,d)}$
> $Proof:$ Let $r=ord(x^m).$ We'll show $r\mid \frac{d}{(m,d)}$ and vice versa.
> * ${} \implies {}$ $(x^m)^{\frac{d}{(m,d)}}=1^{\frac{m}{(m,d)}}=1.$
> * $\impliedby$ $1=(x^m)^r=x^{mr}=x^{dx}\implies d\mid mr\implies \frac{d}{(m,d)}\mid\frac{m}{(m,d)}r\implies \frac{d}{(m,r)}\mid r.$
